AMAL AL KHALEEJ (AMALAK) — BRAND BOARD REFERENCE
=====================================

BRAND NAME
----------
Amal Al Khaleej (AMALAK)
Interior design & contracting — Dubai, UAE


COLOR PALETTE
-------------
#606060 — charcoal grey (body text, secondary UI, structure)
#F2F2F2 — off-white / light grey (page background, light sections)
#C00000 — signal red (primary accent, CTAs, highlights — carried over from the existing brand mark)
#DDD9C3 — warm khaki / stone (secondary accent, warm contrast band, card fills)


DERIVED NEUTRALS
----------------
(tints/shades of the four brand colours above, for large fields — not new brand colours)

#FFFFFF — paired with #F2F2F2 for card surfaces
#EDE8D8 — slightly deeper #DDD9C3, for section bands


FONTS
-----
Primary display / headline font:
  Fraunces (modern serif with sharp, architectural detailing)

Secondary / body font:
  Inter

Font usage:
  Use Fraunces for headlines, hero titles, and section titles — gives the
  brand a confident, design-led feel without tipping into decorative/luxury.
  Use Inter for body copy, navigation, buttons, forms, captions and UI text.


FONT FALLBACK STACKS
--------------------
For Fraunces:
  font-family: "Fraunces", "Georgia", "Times New Roman", serif;

For Inter:
  font-family: "Inter", -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif;


DESIGN STYLE
------------
Style:        Modern interior design & contracting studio
Mood:         Confident, precise, structural, warm-neutral, professional
UI details:   Sharp/soft-square corners (not overly rounded), generous
              whitespace, strong grid alignment, red used sparingly as a
              precision accent (underlines, numerals, CTA buttons, icons),
              khaki used for warm contrast bands and card fills
Avoid:        Overly playful shapes, neon colours, cluttered layouts,
              anything that reads as generic stock-template design

Visual references:
  - Clean architectural photography with strong horizontal/vertical lines
  - Grid-based portfolio presentation
  - Minimal, confident typography hierarchy
  - Red used the way a red pen or blueprint stamp is used: sparing, precise
